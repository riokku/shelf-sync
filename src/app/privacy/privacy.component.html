Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Studio Rio ("Studio Rio," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with ShelfSync (the "Service"), an inventory and task management application for organizations. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account or use the Service, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, nickname, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider, never in plain text), and a profile avatar you choose from a preset list.
  • Organization information — the organization you create or join, your role within it (admin, manager, or staff), and your membership approval status.
  • Content you or your organization add — inventory items and their details (names, descriptions, categories, locations, quantities, prices, supplier information, barcodes, photos), tasks, checkout/assignment records, and activity history. This content is entered by you and your organization's members, not collected from you directly.
  • Camera access, only if you use the barcode/QR scanning feature — your browser asks your permission first, video is processed locally on your device to read a code, and we never transmit or store camera footage. Only the resulting scanned value is saved, as the relevant item's barcode field.
  • Error and diagnostic logs — if the Service encounters an unexpected error while you're using it, we log technical details (like the error message and what you were doing) to help us fix bugs. When you're signed in, this log is linked to your account and organization; if it happens before you sign in, it isn't linked to anyone.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including keeping your organization's data separate and secure from every other organization's;
  • Authenticate you and enforce role-based access within your organization;
  • Send you Service-related communications, such as password reset emails or membership approval notices;
  • Diagnose and fix technical problems; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Cookies and local storage

The Service stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage: an authentication token that keeps you signed in, and your light/dark display preference. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

4. How we share information

We share information with the service providers that host and run ShelfSync on our behalf, currently Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage) and Cloudflare (hosting and content delivery). These providers process data only as needed to provide their services to us. We don't share your information with anyone else, except when required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Studio Rio, our users, or others.

Other members of your organization can see the account information and content visible to their role within the Service (for example, an admin or manager can see who checked out an item, or who requested a task transfer) — this sharing is a normal part of how a shared organizational workspace functions, not a disclosure to a third party.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account and organization remain active. An organization's admin can export their organization's data and permanently delete the organization at any time from Manage → Danger Zone; a deleted organization enters a 30-day recovery window before it and all of its data are permanently purged. You can also request deletion of your individual account by contacting us at the address below.

6. Data security

Your organization's data is isolated from every other organization's at the database level (row-level security policies enforce this on every request), and passwords are never stored in plain text. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

7. Your rights and choices

You can review and update most of your account information at any time from the Account page. You can request a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us below — some information may be retained where we have a legal obligation or legitimate business reason to do so.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly (for example, by email or an in-app notice).

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at chris@studiorioconsulting.com.
