Help & FAQ

Answers to common "how do I..." questions about ShelfSync.

Getting started

How do I invite my team?

Go to Manage > Team and copy the invite link near the top of the page. Anyone who signs up with it joins your organization automatically, as a pending request — an admin still needs to approve them under "Pending join requests" before they can see or do anything.

How do I add my first inventory item?

Admins and managers can create items from Manage > Inventory's "Create item" tab. Which fields show on that form (and which columns show on the Inventory table) can be trimmed down by an admin under Manage > Settings.

How do I create a task?

Admins and managers can create and assign tasks to anyone from Manage > Tasks. Everyone can see and update the status of tasks assigned to them from the Tasks page.

Inventory

What's the difference between card and table view?

Both show the same items — card view is a browsable gallery, table view is a sortable grid. Switch between them with the toggle above the item list on the Inventory page. An admin controls which optional columns the table view shows under Manage > Settings > Data.

What's the difference between a single quantity and tracking by container/box?

A single-quantity item just tracks one number for how much is left. A container/box- tracked item instead splits its stock into individually-editable boxes (e.g. 5 boxes of 20), each with its own location — useful when you need to know exactly which box or pallet has stock. You choose which way to track an item when creating it.

How do I discard damaged, lost, or expired stock?

Open the item's detail popup and use the "Discard" button. You'll pick a quantity (and, for a container-tracked item, which box) plus one or more reasons — discarding permanently reduces the item's stock and is logged to its activity history.

How do I retire an item?

Once an item's remaining quantity hits zero, anyone can request retiring it from its detail popup. Depending on your organization's Settings > Workflow configuration, that either needs an admin/manager's approval or takes effect immediately. A retired item is hidden from the default Inventory view.

What does locking an item do?

Admins and managers can lock an item from its detail popup to stop anyone else from editing it — useful while you're in the middle of correcting its data. Only an admin or manager can unlock it again.

Can I export my inventory data?

Admins and managers can export a CSV of every item (active, pending, and retired) from the "Export" button on Manage > Inventory, with each item's full activity history included.

Reservations & orders

What's a reservation?

A reservation books a quantity of an item's stock for a future date range (e.g. for an upcoming event), so it doesn't get double-booked. It's separate from checking an item out — nothing about its stock or checked-out status changes until someone actually picks the reservation up. Manage reservations from Manage > Reservations.

What's a restock order, and what happens when I mark one received?

An order is a restock request placed against an item's linked supplier, managed from Manage > Orders. Marking an order received automatically adds its quantity back to a single-quantity item's stock — a container/box-tracked item just gets marked received, since you'll still need to add or update a box yourself with where the new stock actually is.

Tasks

How do I hand off a task to someone else?

Open the task and offer a transfer to another approved team member — it stays in your queue until they accept it. They can also decline it, which leaves it with you unchanged.

What do the task statuses mean?

To do, In Progress, and Done — the assignee (or an admin/manager) can move a task between any of them from its detail popup.

Team & roles

What can each role do?

Staff can browse inventory, manage their own tasks, and request things like retirement or a task transfer. Managers get access to most of the Manage hub — inventory, tasks, team, orders, reservations, reports. Admins get everything managers do, plus billing, the danger zone, and site-wide settings.

How do I see who's online?

Manage > Team shows a small pulsing dot on anyone currently online, or "Last seen..." otherwise. The header also shows a live count of how many teammates are online right now, next to your organization's name.

Notifications

What shows up in the bell icon?

A task assigned directly to you, a task transfer offered to you, an inventory retirement request needing admin/manager approval, and a new join request needing admin approval. The same four events can also send an email, unless an admin has turned that category off under Manage > Settings > Workflow.

Settings & branding (admin only)

Where do I customize the theme and logo?

Manage > Settings's Style tab has a color theme picker and a logo upload — both apply site-wide, even before signing in.

Where do I control which fields and columns show?

The Data tab lets you trim which optional fields appear on the "Create item" form and which optional columns the Inventory page's table view shows, plus the approved category/physical-location/discard-reason option lists.

Where do I turn off retirement approval, bulk edit, or a specific email notification?

All under the Workflow tab — retirement approval and bulk edit are each a single toggle, and email notifications have their own toggle per event kind.

Account

How do I change my avatar or switch between light and dark mode?

Both are on your Account page.

How do I reset my password?

Use "Forgot password?" on the login screen to request a reset link by email.
